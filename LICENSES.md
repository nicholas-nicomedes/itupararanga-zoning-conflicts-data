# Licensing, attribution, and third-party data

This repository contains a combination of source-derived geospatial information, third-party data, and research-derived analytical products.

A single repository-wide license should not be interpreted as transferring or superseding rights associated with the original governmental or third-party data sources.

## Source-derived datasets

The following datasets contain spatial information obtained, reconstructed, standardized, or derived from external institutional sources:

- `APAitupararanga`
- `PM`
- `PDM`
- `UCT`

Rights, attribution requirements, and conditions associated with the underlying source information remain subject to the terms established by their respective data providers and institutions.

Users should consult `SOURCES.md` and the original providers before redistributing or applying these data in contexts subject to specific licensing or legal requirements.

## Research-derived analytical products

The following datasets were produced through the analytical procedures described in the associated peer-reviewed study:

- `ConflPMPDM`
- `ClassAdequacaoUCT`

These layers represent scientific analytical products rather than official governmental classifications.

The definitive reuse license for these research-derived products will be specified before publication of dataset version 1.0.0, after confirming compatibility with the terms governing the underlying source datasets.

## Repository documentation

The README, data dictionary, source documentation, and other descriptive metadata were prepared for scientific documentation and reproducibility.

## Important note

Public availability of a file does not, by itself, replace the licensing or attribution requirements associated with its original source.

Users are responsible for respecting the rights and conditions associated with third-party data and normative documents.
