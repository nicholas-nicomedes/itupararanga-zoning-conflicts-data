## [1.0.0] - YYYY-MM-DD

### Added

- Initial public release of the Itupararanga zoning conflicts geospatial dataset.
- `APAitupararanga` spatial layer.
- `PDM` municipal zoning layer.
- `PM` Management Plan zoning layer.
- `ConflPMPDM` normative compatibility layer.
- `UCT` land-use and land-cover layer.
- `ClassAdequacaoUCT` analytical land-use compatibility layer.
- Consolidated GeoPackage containing all analytical layers.
- Web-optimized GeoJSON previews.
- Dataset documentation and citation metadata.
